Agentforce · Manufacturing Cloud

Sales Agreement
Agent.

A ready-to-deploy Agentforce employee agent that takes AEs through the annual pricing cycle on Sales Agreements. Every org-specific rule is a setting, so you can reshape it for any org.

Get the repo → Setup guide

Install in 4 steps

  1. Clone the repogit clone https://github.com/salesforce-misc/MACGZ-salesagreementagent.git
  2. Deploy to your orgsf project deploy start -x manifest/package.xml
  3. Publish & activate the agentsf agent publish authoring-bundle --api-name Sales_Agreement_Agent
  4. Set your rules in the Sales Agreement Agent Setting record.

Needs Manufacturing Cloud + Agentforce. Full commands are in the README.

8 things it does

One agent for the whole pricing cycle. The AE describes the step; the agent picks the capability.

1 · Agreement setup
01

Start the pricing work

Finds the account and its plans, then logs the pricing Account Plan Objective.

Try“I’m starting pricing work for <customer>.”
02

Create or clone the agreement

Builds a Draft agreement from an eligible opportunity, or clones the current one into the next period.

Try“Clone the current agreement into next year.”
03

Apply the approved increase

Previews the change on every product and month, and commits only after you say yes.

Try“Apply the approved 4% across all products.”
04

Track the agreement

Shows planned vs. actual volume and the products that deviate most.

Try“How is this agreement tracking against plan?”
2 · Customer conversation
3 · Close-out
05

Prepare for the conversation

Coaches you step by step on what changed and how the increase ties to the customer’s priorities.

Try“Help me prepare for the pricing conversation with <customer>.”
06

Practice the conversation

Role-plays a realistic customer, stays in character, then gives you coaching feedback.

Try“Let me practice the pricing conversation for <customer>.”
07

Price list & signature

Shares the agreement link, then walks you through Generate Document and Send For Signature on the record.

Try“Walk me through sending the price list for signature.”
08

Close out the cycle

Completes your open tasks and closes the pricing objective, then tells you what it closed.

Try“Close out my pricing objective for <customer>.”
